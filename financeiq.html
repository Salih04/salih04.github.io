FinanceIQ: an evaluation harness that audited its own result

The published baseline reported IC +0.150 (p = 0.017), but used annual statements that were not public at the prediction date. After enforcing point-in-time availability, the baseline was IC +0.031 (p = 0.63). Both evaluations remain published for provenance; the corrected estimate shows no evidence of a large predictive edge in this sample.

02 · method

Walk-forward, with the null attached

Features of year T predict the return of year T+1; each test year (2023, 2024, 2025) is predicted only by models trained on earlier target years. The metric is the within-year Spearman rank correlation (IC), pooled over test years and compared with a null built by permuting returns within each year. Six ML models are corrected for multiplicity; three rank baselines are reported beside them.

03 · audit

The number that did not survive

The published evaluation had one nominally significant result: an equal-weight baseline with pooled IC +0.150 (p = 0.017). An audit in October 2026 asked when each feature could have been known. Year-T fundamentals turned out to be the full fiscal-year statements — matching published annual figures to the million lira — and those are filed weeks into the year whose return they predict.

The audit also found market value computed from a back-adjusted price times a historical share count, and pre-listing rows carrying a vendor placeholder return. The published results were kept byte-for-byte.

04 · correction

Point-in-time, checked by code

  • timestamps
    Every feature records when its information became public, in a machine-readable registry. A guard rejects any value whose timestamp is after the prediction time; tests inject future-available features and the original design and check that each is rejected.
  • cutoff
    Exact filing dates exist for only a sample, so statements count as public from their statutory deadline. Signal and return start on the first trading day after 31 May; an end-of-March cutoff was rejected because the FY2023 deadline was 20 May 2024.
  • market value
    Rebuilt on one adjustment basis (price as quoted × shares at the time), or excluded where the share record and the split history disagree.
  • registration
    The protocol was committed before its results were computed, as a dated amendment that leaves the original pre-registration untouched. It is post-hoc: designed after the audit.

05 · reading

What the result means

  • supported
    In this sample and design, the corrected evaluation found no evidence of a large reproducible predictive edge: baseline IC +0.031 (p = 0.63), no ML model distinguishable from chance.
  • power
    The design can reliably detect only |IC| above about 0.19. Smaller effects remain outside its detection power: a null here means no large edge, not no edge.
  • scope
    One high-inflation period in nominal lira, three test years, and an 81-company universe chosen in 2026: survivorship is unresolved.
  • not claimed
    Market efficiency, useless fundamentals, or that a better model would fail.
